How a lesson works

Read it, watch it, build it, prove it.

  1. 1

    Read the idea

    The left page explains one idea in plain words with a picture, key words and a question to predict.

  2. 2

    Watch it move

    Press play and a warm narrator reads along while the 3D model acts it out: blocks regroup, cakes split, clocks tick.

  3. 3

    Build it yourself

    Drag coins into a tray, swing a clock hand, stretch a garden fence. Kids touch the model, not a worksheet.

  4. 4

    Win the stamp

    The Practice spread has quick questions with flaps to check, then a 5-round game. Win all five to stamp the lesson.

Inside the book

Every expectation, page by page.

Each lesson is tagged with the Ontario curriculum codes it covers, so teachers can match it to their plans and parents can see what the class is working on.

Open the book

For families

  • Opens in the browser on any phone, tablet or computer.
  • Every page can be read aloud, so younger readers can follow on their own.
  • Stars and stamps are saved on your device, so kids pick up where they left off.

For teachers

  • Each lesson lists its curriculum codes, from B1.1 to F1.1.
  • Put the Learn spread on the board for the whole class, then use the Practice games in centres.
  • Kids can replay any Practice game, and most games make new puzzles each time.

About

SpatialSprout 3D

SpatialSprout 3D makes interactive 3D books for students in Ontario. The idea is simple: some things click when you can pick them up and turn them around. Our books put that into every lesson.

The books in The Interactive Classroom series are written by M. A. Mafodh. Grade 3 and Grade 4 Math are open now. Next come the other grades, then science, language and social studies.
